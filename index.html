How it picks

For each possible guess, the app imagines every word that could still be the answer and works out which colours you would see. A good guess splits the words into lots of small groups, so whatever colours come back, few words are left.

That is called information (or entropy), measured in bits. A guess that could win right now gets a small bonus. Words that are more common count more, because puzzle answers are usually everyday words.

About

Word Solver . Find the best next guess in five-letter word puzzles, and every word you can make from your tiles. Works offline. MIT licence.

Word lists

Words come from the ENABLE word list (Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon, public domain): 172,823 words. How common each five-letter word is comes from wordfreq by Robyn Speer (data CC BY-SA 4.0). This app is not made by or connected with any word game or its makers, and it doesn't use any game's official answer list.
